## Four inevitable questions

**Is the prediction validated?**  
No — backtested on synthetic longitudinal data calibrated to published trauma-recovery trajectories. MAE and lead time are stated. Prospective validation design is ready.

**How is it explainable?**  
The score is a weighted composite; the explanation is the same arithmetic (`score_contributions`), not a second model asked to rationalise the first. Show the waterfall.

**False positives?**  
Every alert routes to a human. The system never acts autonomously. Thresholds are tunable.

**Privacy?**  
PII redacted pre-inference with a live counter, enforced granular consent, hash-chained audit, retention limits. Show `/audit/verify` and the consent path.
